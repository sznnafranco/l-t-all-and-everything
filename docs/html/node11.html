Taking the Blame

This is a very good and useful game to play, especially if you are playing in with others who know about and understand it. In this scenario it can be quite hilarious. There are also potential pitfalls to this game, so tread with caution. You have to make sure that you are not saying things that are going to upset others. This is not nice, and we don't do it except in situations where the other person knows what is going on and has agreed to it. Even if this is the case you still need to use much caution. If your wife, husband, children, or other family or friends are around, if you offend them too much, even though they agreed to the game, you may be very, very sorry. We are trying to play Games that will be useful and fun for all involved, and it is useful to push people a certain amount sometimes. To repeat, when there are people involved that you care about, and would not like to be separated from, be very, very, careful. Along those same lines, here in the latter part of the second decade of the twenty first century, you need to be very careful about legal liability, both civil and criminal. If you are living in the USA at this time, then you know about the potential risks. If you are from somewhen different, a little research into the legal shenanegins of this time may be interesting.

As its name indicates, this game is about taking the blame and how this can benefit all involved, save countless hours, days, weeks, years, and decades of sensless fussing and fighting. Rather than spending the evening arguing with, ignoring, and pouting at one another, wouldn't it be nice to get it over with quickly and play a game that is much more fun and useful? So, let's say you have a situation where someone's cookie is gone, and they say “Who ate my cookie?” and you immediately reply “I did!” Well, this is going to pretty much take the wind out of the sails of the argument, and as long as you reply calmly and reasobable, it will dissapear pretty soon. Notice that by simply taking the blame you have freed both yourself and the other person from a whole pile of bad smelling do-do. Now, let's look at another scenario where another person is involved, the one who actually ate the cookie. When the one who actually ate the cookie hears your confession, they will probably be very surprised and resieved. Between you and the accuser, things are largely the same, but since there is another person present, the accuser will probably be a lot less likely to make a fuss about it. In this case all three of yu have been freed from whatever amount of silliness would have otherwise ensued.
