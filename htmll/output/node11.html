Born Games

Pay attention to your breath while you eat game.

Pay attention to every part of breath in every bite of every meal. Bites include drinks too.

This is one of the most important games and involves bringing the Born center, which is represented by the diamonds in the diagrams, into our quest to advance our spiritual journey. It is not so easy for some people to actually do this Game. There was once an hour long meeting of a group of “spiritual Masters” who sat around for the whole hour commiserating about how hard this Game was. Not one of them had actually ever been able to remember to try it. It is obvious that no Game is going to help you unless you actually learn to play it. We have this opportunity several times each day, which makes it a unique chance to drive, bit by bit, our progress along our own personal, inner (spiritual) path. We will start out by looking at some possible ways to remind ourselves to play this Game.

Imagination, when used properly, can be one of our most powerful and helpful human possesions. In this case you can imagine different reminding factors, or ways in which you could help yourself to remember to pay attention to your breath every single time you eat. Here are some examples to get you started. Put notes on things, your refrigerator, your wallet if you pay for lunch, the sandwich in your lunch box or the olive oil you put on your shrimp and the bag of shrimp in the freezer. Switch your watch to the other hand, so that when you check to see if its lunch time it reminds you to practice this game. You could buy yourself a new piece of jewelry just for this purpose, so that whenever you see the item, it reminds you of what you need to do. Write WATCH BREATH on the front or back of your hand, make up and menorize a poem about the Game, add in some magical unicorns, frolicing fairies, and beautiful butterflies, and find someone to share it with!

Another way to help remember and play this Game is to understand and see its potential power and usefulness to our journey. This relates to the second being obligation striving: To have a constant and unflagging instinctive need for self perfection in the sense of being. It is necessary to use all our cunning, wit, and attention to make this beast serve our purposes, to build up that which is useful and strip away that which is harmful. As always, our first concern must be the ordinary negative emotions which we have absorbed throughout our lives from so many different sources. Things like guilt, fear, shame, self pride, and anger will surly arise. Energy directed to negative emotions may involve a wrong use of sex energy, in which case it will all be directed in one direction and our actions will be taken to extremes. In this case the energy is lost to us. We must learn to see negative emotions like these and go against them by not expressing them and transforming them into positive emotions.

So, what could be useful in this Born center? Having spent our whole lives eating several times a day, it is easy to see that there is a huge amount of energy involved. As we learn to channel this energy away from negative emotions we can start to see more and more about ourselves. If we are accustomed to experience pleasant emotions while eating, a similar process of wasting energy will mechanically ensue, although the amounts of energy are generally not as great. As we learn to go against these mechanical processes, we will have extra energy which can be directed more and more subtle insights that will not be always headed towards extremes. There will be many different parts of ourselves to which this energy can be applied. There will always be more and more little things which can be improved. It is also important to note that not only will this energy no longer be wasted, it will actually produce more and better energy. All of the energy spent on negative and pleasant emotions is wasted. The energy spent on this kind of active inner work gives results that you can never lose.

Another factor to consider here is the fact that these opportunities arise several times a day and always will. When you attach this beneficial game to this event of eating, you have a base that can not be equalled in any other way. How much effort do you think that those “spiritual Masters” who could not even implement this most simple Game were able to devote to other more complex, powerful, and useful Games? This Game is just the beginning of your spiritual (inner) journey. As you progress along your path you will discover things that you can not even imagine!

Once you start playing this Game, it would be a good idea to observe it over time and try to do it better, or just experiment with different way to play. Your first goal should be to learn to play this game every time you eat. After that you want to be able to play the Game throughout the whole meal. There is a big differece between watching ome breath at the beginning or end of the meal and practicing thouroughly through the whole meal. If you can get someone to play the Game with you this is probably better than playing alone. An exception to this might be someone who is very annoying. In this case, however, you can play another Game which yu should be playing all the time anyway, Expanding your ability to bear annoying events. This Game can be played at any time, not just at meal time, and its not that hard to play the two games simultaneously, at least at lunch time! You can look at and experiment with many thhings such as different foods, but as long as you are playing the Game continuously and well, the details are probably not all that important.
