Intellectual Games

Emotional Power Moments Game

What is this game doing in the Intellectual Games section? Shouldn't it be in the Emotional Games section? That is a very good question with a very important answer. This Game is in the Intellectual Games section because it involves memorizing a list of things, which is a very important way to improve your Intellectual center.

many ends of the stick Here we try to find more possibilities when the jack of spades presents only one or two possibilities. This will give us more ways to respond to people and situations. It also reduces binary thinking which is needed for our negative emotions, which we need to control and eliminate. It also ties in to the idea of wrong use of sex energy in the lower centers.

Related Associations Game from chapter 2 vvov.

memorize game

Memorizing material from the games is a necessary part of being successful in the Games. Different people have different abilities in this area, and people for whom it is easy should probably memorize more words than those for whom it is dificult. Some good candidates would be the prayer, the obligonian strivings with the introduction, the 8, 10, 12 makes 30 passage. It should always be remembered that rote memorization, while very important, is not the ultimate goal, but a way to help you towards that goal. In the end you want to come to the point where you can bring up an idea immediately without even thinking about it at the appropriate time and situation, and be able to paraphrase it, act in accordance with it, and convey it to others or see others conveying it whether words are involved or not. Do you think that this might tend to involve the Higher Mental Center?
