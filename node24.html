Prayer

Many people, both the religious and the not religious, have considered questions like: If there is a God, and he is good and all-knowing and all-powerful, them why does he let so much evil go on in the world, why doesn't he answer our prayers? These Games are primarily about finding our own inner (spiritual) path. If we want to help others, we must realize that in order to do that, we must first realize that we need to change ourselves and then look to help others to do the same. So here are two aspects of prayer that need to be addressed. What are we praying for and how do we pray? If we realize that spiritual writings are about spiritual (inner) things, then we will realize that we should be praying for change inside of ourselves. Once we realize this we can start to practice praying and see if it works. Like any other games, it takes practice to learn how to pray, and if your “practice” consists of doing it completely wrong, you won't get any where. If you spend years, or even decades, on your knees praying for an end to hunger in the world, something which has surley been done repeatedly in history, it's like trying to learn to play tennis with a ping pong paddle. (Probably worse because whatever nonsense you are attempting with the ping pong paddle, you may at least get some exercise!) It is also necessary to know what to pray for. If you ask God to make you a better at praing than your friend, nothing good can come of it, and the best case scenario is that you get no answer. If you ask God to help you learn to pray better and properly, and to show you how to help your friend and recieve help from your friend, and you do it having studied and understood the cards diagrams, and you do it with the tenacity and determination of a mother defending her child, you will get good results.

Another aspect of praying that we need to understand and master is that it works a whole lot better when you have help. By striving together we can accompoish much more than the sum of what we cam accomplish separately. In order to demonstrate (verify) this, try to play the pay attention to your breath while eating Game alone for a while. It is definately possible to practice this game alone and to make some, perhaps even a lot, of progress. Now get a friend or companion and try it together for a few months. You can certainly, even only as a thought experiment, see that this would work much better! Now, let's look at some further refinements. If you and your friend had lunch together every day you would get much better results than you could get alone. If you lived together and practiced together at every meal, you would get still better results. And if you had a group all practicing together, the results would be still better yet. It is important to note that once again this is going to be a geometric or arithmatic progression, rather than a linear one.

All of this would continue for some time until, at some point, the fussing, fighting and arguing started, and then that would be the end of it, right? Well, hopefully not, because these are just the kind of negative emental manefestations with which these games are designed to deal. It is, so to speak, where the rubber meets the road, the women separated from the girls, etc. etc. etc. This is actually your golden opportunity, because these types of negative emotions are where the largest part of our energy is expended. While you can save a lot of energy by transforming pleasant emotions, unpleasant and negative emotions are where the vast majority of our energy is directed, and therefore where we have the greatest chance to save this energy and redirect it towards propeling us along our inner (spiritual) path. We must first learn how to stop expressing these negative emotions, and simultaneously learn how to transform them into positive emotions, which can only exist on the spiritual level. There are several things you need to keep in mind here. The intellectual center, the first tame elephant, the jack and queen of spades, can help out here if it has learned about the Games and the diagrams, but it is going to be late to the party because it is too slow to stop these negative emotions in real time. It is like trying to use the intellectual center to direct every movement while walking down stairs. Too slow. The emotional center is supposed to work very much faster than the born and learned centers. When it is ensnared in these types of negative emotions, it can only work at about the same speed as the physical level centers do. This is why we need to have all our forces ready and be constantly exercising and practicing with them. Intentional effort, directed with the idea of moving yourself along your personal inner (spititual) path, is what gets the kings out of bed and starts to strengthen them. These kings will eventually start to give us more and more and longer and longer contact with the spititual level. And when we function from the spititual level, we are much faster than any of the lower centers, and eventually most (maybe even all?) ofn these pesky little negative emotions will be swatted away like little flies.

Now, you may be wondering, how did we get from prayer to playing a game? This is another aspect of praye that is not very well known. Most people, whether religious or not, think of prayer as something that you do on sundays in church, or maybe when you go to bed at night. There is nothing wrong with praying in church or when you go to bed, it's kind of a more the merrier situation. It is a valid and important way of looking at the games to say that their objective is to teach you to pray better and more. Two Games immediately come to mind. If you take communion in your church, can you practice the pay attention to your breath Game at that time? There is another Game that is apropos in this context, one that is called Lying still for one hour. This is a good thing to do every night when you go to sleep. And no, you don't have to give up an hour of your sleep time. In these Games we always try to do things that are smart and clever, and disrupting your sleep is neither. The idea, on the contrary, is to get to sleep faster and better as you progress on your inner (spiritual) path. So after whatever prayer you decide to practice when you lie down to sleep, try lying still for an hour until you get to sleep, and you should find that you get to sleep faster and sleep better as you progress on your inner (spiritual) journey. There is always a Game you can play, and the more you add to your repertiore the better will be your progress.

It will also be very unwieldy and clumsy, our language does not contain this type of words and ideas. (This is because we do not contain this type of words and ideas.) On the other hand, if it is written so as to highlight the idea that God is external, it will tend to reduce these malficient aspects of ourselves. They exist, to some extent, in everyone, but our lower, base impulses tend to keep us from seeing or interacting with them. Emental refers to our thoughts and emotions which are very closly connected. See the diagram Man as a 3 Story Being for basic info about this. Wherever you are when you start your inner jouney, it will involve constant evaluation and reevaluation of what you are, and what you want, need, and have the duty to become. This is not set in stone, but an excellent starting point, probably something that would be very advantageous for memorization. 0) Thank you for getting me through this little financial rough spot, small glitch in my relationship, insignificant food issue, etc.

This one is optional and eventually you may no longer need it and thus remove it from your prayer. The ideas are to identify whatever problems you have in life at the moment and put them in perspective, erring on the side of making them smaller than they seem. Our problems always seem a lot bigger than they really are due to the negative emotions in the emotional center, which come from the jack and queen of hearts. Real fear comes mostly from the born center, the jack and queen of diamonds. We want to learn to distinguish between these and let the diamonds guide us to solve our problems, while going against the false fear that comes from the hearts. We also want to understand that the problem is already fixed, which it is for you while you are lying in bed. There is nothing you can do until morning, and staying up all or half the night will be VERY counter productive. You need to get a good night's sleep so you have a clear head in the morning and be able to better deal with your situation.

The idea of this part is to see whatever bad stuff is going on as small and already over with. It comes first so that you can afterwards concentrate on the important stuff, which is what is inside you. External things can always dissapear, and eventually they will, one way or another. We will keep the progress we make on our inner (spiritual) path for at least the rest of our lives.

This is related to the idea that Higher Centers are fully functional but Man, in his present state, has little or no contact with, or control or knowledge of them. As we grow in life we aquire much wrong stuff, ideas, feelings, and habits, to name a few. Over the course of our lives we gradually loose contact with our higher centers. These parts of us are well aware that they will have to die if we are successful in our quest to follow our true inner (spiritual) path by means of the Games. They control us through negative emotions like fear, so they would love for us to imagine that the Games are taking us to a strange, evil, foreign place, rather than BACK to our true home.

This is kind of a generic version of this prayer line, and a rather advanced version. It is good for everyone to leave in their prayer and contemplate from time to time. It will be especially interesting and useful to notice how your perception and understanding of this idea changes over months and years. You should add as many people to the end of this prayer line according to your currint situation in life. If you have a problem with anyone, you should ask to forgive and help them. In most cases you want to make up, i.e. make ammends in person too. If people have tried to kill you or have you killed however, you should probably stay away from them. You really need to forgive them in your own heart if you want to make good progress on your own personal inner (spiritual) path. This is a hard row to hoe, however, and you will probably end up having to go through many “layers of the onion” before you get it really finished. Our ability to decieve ourselves in things like this is truly prodigious. This has to do with buffers, which are amoung our most difficult obstacles, and which we must clearly see and overcome in order to “become as little children.”

3) Show me how to keep the Amanon, what you want me to become, and teach me how to get there.

The Amanon are like little angles we have inside of ourselves that need to pe protected from the lower parts of ourselves. This is what it means to keep the Amanon, to protect them from bad things inside of ourselves. They live on the Spiritual level, in the Higher Emotional center. There are other beings inside of us other Game pieces, which reside on the lower levels. They form a group around our interest in and desire to follow the Games, or in other words our unique, personal, inner (spiritual) path. Since these lower Game pieces live on the lower levels, they cannot see so well what is above them, on the Spiritual level. The Amanon live in and come from the Spiritual level, so they are better able to show us what we need to become, and help us figure out how to get there. Keeping the Amanon involves fighting negative emotions, and going against our lower, most base, sexual desires.

4) Show me how to keep the Amanon, how to know, love, understand, see, and cherish them; and how to nurture them so they grow and multiply amazingly quickly and well.

Here the Amanon pop up again, but this time in a slightly different context. We envision our whole spiritual journey, from the Emental level through to the top of the Spiritual level as a five step process. We start in the Intellectual center (know) and proceed thoough Emotional center (love), Higher Emotional center (understand), Higher Mental center (see), and finally to The Crown (cherish). The Crown is a conception of God which is the absolute peak of what we, as human beings can glimpse even with the help of our higher centers. Glimpse, however we can, and we should do so every now and then, and watch how our conception of The Crown changes over days, months, years, and decades. Ask, and ye shalt recieve, knock, and it shall be opened. Here we are asking whatever our current conception of God is to show us something better, the best that we can currently recieve. By making one little baby step at a time over and over, with a determination to never give up, you will come to places that you can't yet imagine!

In the second part of this line of the prayer we segue from looking at the Amanon as a path to seeing them as individual Game pieces. We have Game pieces on all levels of our being, and in all centers. They can be divided up into groups in various ways. One particularly useful way to group the Game pieces is according to their interest in an usefulness to the Games. Imagine this, since you are reading this, the information you are being exposed to is entering into your Emental level. This will cause certain mechanical reactions in the jack and queen of both hearts and spades. You probably have some that are interested, think this is cool, others that are skeptical, and still more that are downright antagonistic. If the first two groups can band together and survive for a while, over time others will notice and come join the party. This is a necessary first step, and eventually it should lead to the appearance of the kings, which can open the way towards higher centers.

The possibilities stretch out to the horizon and beyond. After you get started you will soon be able to keep yourself headed in the right direction, with a little help from your friends, of course. Now that we know that we exist of three levels and need to advance on all of them, it is a good idea to pay attention to this and try to get what we need. In these Games we have to be (and become) sly men, always using every rescource available to us, and constantly pushing our limits, which is a very good way to discover and develop new skills, talents, and tactics.

It is important to realize that no one really knows what happens after we die. Anyone who claims they do is suffering from having sexual energy run amok on their Emental level, a condition which is fairly easy to recognize by observing that someone is taking an extreme position in relation to a particular issue or subject, and insisting on a particular, or sometimes only two possible outcomes, solutions, possibilities etc. Such a person is stuck in the jack and queen of spades, which can't count past two. This state is necessary for a host of malficient negative emotions which we in the games are in the process of eliminating. So watch out for this yes or no thinking in yourself and others, and learn to extricate yourself from it. Do you really need to reply to that idiot on social media? Do you really need to jump into that argument at the water fountain, or with your husband or wife? Not engaging in this type of behavior is a valid, useful, and important strategy if you want to advance on your inner (spiritual) path.

As to what happens after you die, this is a fruitful thing to ponder, contemplate, and meditate about, as long as you keep in mind that this is something that has never been verified, and can not be verified at this time. Just because it is written in a book is not a verification that something is true. Just becauase we haven't been able to observe something yet does not mean that it doesn't or can't exist. The amount of stuff that science knows that we don't know, especially in the realms of dark matter, dark energy, and quantum physics, leaves plenty of room for the possibility of some kind of existence after death. Maybe it will turn out that we are some kind of dark matter collectors and when we die we go to the kingdom of dark energy where, if we have collected enough dark matter during our lives, we get a job with the dark energy king!

This has to do with getting rid of buffers, which we must accomplish in order to become as little children, who can see things much more clearly in some ways because they do not yet have these buffers completely established. Buffers keep one part of ourselves from seeing another, contradictory part. This allows us to do all manner of horrible things without feeling too bad about it. We make excuses, self justify, blame it on someone else, say we are just being honest, say they deserved what they got, say our anger is righteous, and even a good thing. We are scared of the most ridiculous imaginary thimgs, while letting the figurative freight train repeatedly demolish us and the symbolic stampede trample us day after day. Here you can easily imagine the jacks and queens of hearts and spades going crazy, running around causing havoc, not only on the Emental level, but the Physical and Spiritual ones as well. But just knowing that all this is going on, and even actually seeing it happen, doesn't necessarily just make it dissappear. This is a very clear and concise formulation of something we need to be able to see, and what we need to learn to do about it. While it is imperative to learn to transform pleasant and unpleasant emotions, from which we will stop a constant trickle of energy loss, learning to stop expressing, and to go against, negative emotions, and to transform them into positive emotions gives the huge amounts of energy that we need to really triumph in the battles!

8) Give me really cool dreams that I can remember, understand, and apply to the Amanon, the Games, and myself.

Dreams can come from any part of ourselves, from any center or part of a center. Mostly they come from the Born, Learned, and Emotional centers. The Intellectual center mostly shuts down when we sleep in our beds. It is interesting to note that these three centers are those that make up the Higher Emotional center. Dreams can also be more or less based on reality. Nightmares are pretty much wholly based on the imaginary fears that exist due to imitation in our Emotional centers, and keep them functioning in a way that ensures we will remain in this hell for our whole lives. The best way to deal with nightmares is to not express negative emotions, to go against them, and to learn to transform them into Positive emations, remembering that Positive emotions only exist in higher centers. As you progress along your own personal inner (spiritual) journey, nightmares will receed and be replaced with pleasant dreams, which might sometimes have a spiritual (inner) meaning from which you can benefit. It should be noted though that it is fairly unusual to make significant discoveries this way, so don't start writing down all your dreams every day because it will waste a lot of time with very little return on your investment. It is, however, a good idea to quickly remember your dreams after you wake up in the morning. It is not uncommon to get little tips about our inner (spirityal) path from our dreams.

There are a couple of characters that you can look out for in your dreams that may be significant. The Higher Emotional center is generally portrayed by girl or woman. She will be very attractive, but not in an overtly sexual way. She may show you things, or try to get you to complete tasks for her. If she talks her messages will be short and to the point, but not necessarily easy to understand without some time and reflection. She may show you several scenarios which demonstrate the same point in different ways. If there are bad or scary people involved, they will not manifest as a nightmare per se, i.e. scaring you so much that you wake up, but will be an interesting, albeit not entirely pleasant part of the story or scene. These bad guys can manifest from either first person or third person point of view. Either way, you can be sure that there is some unbecomming aspect of your being that she wants you to concentrate on with your Games. This kind of dream that has a kind of uncomfortagle feel to it, but is not a nightmare, can also happen without a female character in it. Here is a takeaway: Higher Emotional center, if personified in a dream, is likely to be a female character to whom you feel drawn, but in an emental way as opposed to a sexual way. Unpleasant characters are reflections of things in yourself that you put effort (kings of centers) into in your Game playing.

Then there is Silent Man, who is a representation of Higher Mental center. He doesn't talk much, and when he does it may be in a foreign, unintellegible language. Sometimes he wears a business suit, sometimes rags. He has all kinds of really, really cool stuff, toys, gardens, machines, caves filled with cool stuff, campsites, and even whole worlds. You just know that there is an amazing organization benind all of his bric-a-brac, but it is just too far above you to understand. Getting a glimpse of things that are far, far beyond our ability to comprehend can remind us of how far we still have to go, show us the wonder awaiting us if we persist, and spur us to make ever more and more, and better and better efforts (Kings).
