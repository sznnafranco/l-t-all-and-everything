Salt is such a basic human need that in Roman times the soldiers were paid in salt, which is where our modern word salary comes from. In modern times there has been much ado about salt, a lot of it could be considered to be perpetrated by people stuck in yes or no thinking, and going way overboard by trying to supress and even ban salt. Salt, or sodium chloride is necessary for all life to continue and thrive. We do, after all, all come from the ocean if you go far enough back in the evolutionary tree! Having proper levels of salt is essential for proper nerv function, fluid balance, muscle activity, and blood pressure. It is also important for overall cellular functioning. If you are making real efforts to fix your diet you should have no chance of consuming too much salt. The amount of salt that our bodies need varies, according to, drum roll please... you guessed it, how much salt you have eaten lately. Luckily, our body has a very finely tuned mechanism to help us figure out how much salt to eat. If you have never known this before it is imperative that you ponder it for a while, and then practice getting the amount of salt in your diet right whenever you eat. All chefs and good cooks know about this, and deal with it in this way. When they are preparing a meal or recipe, they add less salt than the person eating the meal will want. That way, each individual can taste the food, and put on exactly the right amount of salt that their body needs right now, or in other words, salt it to taste. It is impoortant to note that the correct amount of salt does not make the food taste salty, changes it from bland to just right. If your food tastes salty, it means you have put on too much salt.

Another health issue related to salt is goiter, which is a swelling of the thyroid gland which can be caused by iodine deficiency. The relation between goiter and iodine has been well known for around a hundred years, so you should use iodized salt to avoid thie condition.
