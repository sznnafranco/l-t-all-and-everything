Odds and Ends

Smoking Story

There once was a man who was born addicted to alchohol and ciggaretts. He realized that this was the case rather late in his life, when he heard that for most people their first cigarette was a very traumatic experience, whereas for him it had been like going to heaven. This did not surprise him very much because knew that his mother had smoked and drank while she was pregnant with him. He had not started smoking until late in his teens, whereas most of the cool kids had been smoking since middle school. The man's parents had quiet time every day around five pm where children should be seen and not heard, while the adults had their first drinks of the evening. The man's father had told him that if one was going to drink, you might as well get drunk, so this is how the man drank for a couple of decades. At a certain point the man decided to quit drinking and did so for about a decade by going to a lot (3 per day) of AA meetings. The man also quit smoking during this period.

Around the end of this ten year period the man acquired a certain Spiritual Teacher who helped the man out very much. He was a very kind, patient, and intelligent teacher, who had made some amazing discoveries which were very important and useful to those trying to advance along their own inner (spiritual) path. When the man reached a certain purported milestone on his inner (spiritual) journey, the teacher told the man that he could start drinking again. This started the man on a twenty five year oddesy in relation to these two kings, drinking and smoking. (Not to be confused with the four kings which represent our efforts in relation to the Games.) The man had observed over his lifetime certain people who could have a ciggarette or two when they went out at night, and then not smoke the next day, or even later on in the night. (Cigarette adicts secretly hate these people.) The man really liked his drinking and smoking, and therefore decided that, as long as it wasn't putting him in the poor house, or taking food off his children's table, he could allow himself this pleasure. He spent about 25 years trying out different approaches twoards his drinking, and in the end got to the point where he could go out every now and then and monitor his drinking to make sure he didn't go over the legal limit for driving, and when he stayed home he could drink sometimes more or sometimes less, depending on anything or nothing.

It turns out that trying to control these two vices is a lot more dificult than trying to just quit. (If you want to quit any particular bad thing you may be doing, just seek out the appropriate 12 step group. These people are kind, generous, and mostly wise beyond their years.) It is also a very good and important thing to do if you want to suceed in the Games, i.e. advance along your own personal inner pathway. At the same time that the man had been trying to comtrol his vices, he had been trying to improve his diet. It's very dificult to concentrate on your inner (spiritual) journey when you are sitting on or leaning over the toilet because of bad food you have eaten. All of these three things, drinking, smoking, and eating are very tightly tied to our Born center. The urges to eat, drink, and smoke or not at any particular moment are all coming from our body, and are all intertwined with an related to each other. The food journey can be divided into two journies, removing foods journey and the adding foods journey.

IF YOU DON'T REMOVE THE BAD FOODS BEFORE YOU PUT IN THE GOOD ONES, YOU WILL NEVER BE ABLE TO MAKE ANY PROGRESS OR VERIFY ANYTHING HAVING TO DO WITH THE BORN CENTER.

IF YOU DON'T REMOVE THE BAD FOODS BEFORE YOU PUT IN THE GOOD ONES, YOU WILL SEVERLY LIMIT YOURSELF NOT ONLY IN THE BORN CENTER, BUT IN ALL CENTERS, AND WILL MAKE MANY THINGS VERY DIFFICULT OR IMPOSSIBLE TO VERIFY.

The last part of the remove food journey involves trying not to get the poops, i.e. have diarrhea, which of course is commonly and easily caused by eating or drinking the wrong things. It is necessary to learn to pay careful attention to both what goes in and what comes out. The man was getting pretty good at this by this time, having practiced it for many decades.

The man had also seen an online video which mentioned self hypnosis, something he had heard of but never actually tried. The technique, which was briefly outlined in the video basically consisted of telling yourself that you would like to breathe in nice clean fresh air rather than dirty smelly smoke. This made a lot of sense so the man started practicing this whenever he got an urge to smoke and he did not want to smoke. After a couple of weeks it seemed that the practice had little or no effect. Then one morning he had an insight, and was quite certain that it would turn out to be correct. What he needed to do was, instead of imagining fresh air where he was, at the present time, he needed to substitute memories from his childhood, in places that had always been special to him. When he tried this, he immediately knew that it had worked, so he did it for a couple of days just to verify and make sure about it.

The idea behind this whole exercise, which the man had spent a couple of decades trying to perfect, was to be able to, at appropriate intervals, (not every night, but indeterminate other than that) and have whatever number of drinks seemed good, and whatever cigaretts with which seemd appropriate to accompany them. So the man drank most of the first shot, just a little mor quickly than normal, (He sipped the shots and used them for mouthwash as was his custom.) Then he smoked his first cigarette in about two days, and immediately farted out a small amount of clear liquid. It wasn't even enough to get his to pants, so after cleaning himself up he considered the situation. (It is interesting to note that smoking the cigarette wasn't even that wonderful.) What this meant was that the cigarettes were giving him the poops, something he had thought of before, but considered unlikely. This conclusion caused the man to realize that his situation was hilariously ironic: He had just spent decades seeking and actually accomplishing something that was now of very little use to him, because would seldom or never want to smoke badly enough to give himself the poops. So, in order to verify this, the man drank another four shots without smoking, during which time he had no more opooping problems. He then started smoking again, and sure enough got the poops fairly quickly.

So, what can we take from this story? The simplest lesson is a very familiar one, smoking is bad for you so don't do it. This is something that it doesn't seem should require decades of experimentation to verify. Common sense should be able to tell us that inhaling hot smoke into your lungs probably isn't such a good idea. This is a very good example of how buffers can let us do stupid things without admitting that they are stupid. These buffers are always tied to various negative emotions to which we cling desperately, in this case various petty and imaginary fears.

There is something much more interesting hidden here, so let's see what it is! Here is a practical application of the law of three to the task of fulfilling our spiritual (inner) destiny and duty. The self-hypnosis can be seen as a Game, or prayer, we are trying to change something in ourselves. This starts in Intellectual center at the appropriate time, when we want to smoke. Here you could substitute any mechanical manifestation to which you would like to apply the Games. You have to start with small things, because when that guy flips you the bird on the highway, it's too late. (Learning to control our mechanical reactions to this kind of thing is absolutely something we can learn to control.) So let's use a small thing as an example in this case. Let's say you get upset with your child when he leaves his empty water bottles on the table, tather than throwing them away. First, you formulate a statement “I'm not going ot snap at my child about the water bottles” Then you come up with a moment, or a few moments involving your child that have a very strong emotional quality for you. Maybe when they run up to you wanting to be picked up. These can be more general too, maybe how wonderful it was spending the summer with them in the past. We remember these moments because they have connected us with the Higher Emotional center, and we can use them to aid us in our Games. The fact that we are intentionally doing this in real time, in order to traverse our inner (spiritual) landscape, shows us that the king of hearts, which is always involved when we make effort. So we end up with the following: The higher (Higher Emotional center, understanding, desire to help others) blends with the lower (Intellectual center, where we formulated our aim.) in order to actualize the middle (Emotional center, king of hearts, involving effort on our part) and thus becomes either higher for the preceeding lower (Intellectual center) or lower for the suceeding higher. (Higher Emotional center) It's pretty easy to understand that the Higher Emotional center is is above the Emotional center, but perhaps not so clear that the intellectual center is below the Emotional center. Here is the key: The Intellectual center is below the Emotional center as we currently are because of its speed, or more precisely its lack thereof. The Intellectual center is our slowest center. The centers on the Physical level function much faster, and the Emotional center should function faster still, but is probably around the same speed as the Physical level centers in practice. As we play these Games we gain experience, building our Higher Emotional center little by little. We will be able to add many memories to our repetoir of moments we can use as emotional fuel for our journey. Sometimes the memory of crisp, clean country air from our childhood can be the most powerful and wonderful of all!
