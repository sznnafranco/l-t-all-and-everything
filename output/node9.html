The Basic Games

Oot/Oat

The main focus of these Games is to change and improve ourselves. To that end learning to observe ourselves is paramount. The Oot/Oat game is two games for the price of one. Can it get any better than that? Oot is Observe, Oppose, Transform and Oat is Observe, Amplify, Transform. We start out with Oot because most of our time and energy is spent on useless, imaginary, harmful negative emotions. Seeing them, not expressing them, and opposing them has to be at the forefront at all times. We can observe ourselves in the past, present, and future. Of the three, the present is quite the most dificult. We can start out looking at the past, what we have done and how we have treated others during our lifetime. This is the Moral Inventory from the twelve step programs. By becoming familiar with what we have done in the past, we can have a good idea of how we will react in the future. Our mechanical negative Emental Manifestations are reactions to external e ents, the queens of the centers are very predictable and this can help us know how we will react in the future. If we know that someone is foing to do something that anoys us, we can prepare ourselves and try not to react, both on the inside and the outside.

One of the most important features of our observation is impartiality. We need to learn to see things in ourselves without having a negative emotional judgement about it, i.e. We don't want to bring up more negative emotions because of the ones we previously manifested. This just perptuates the normal mechanical flow of all this stuff. We can practice sepatating thoughts from emotions. Learn to observe the Intellectual center apart from the Emotional center. At the same time, we must learn to make honest mental asessments of what we have seen. When we manifest badly we need to recognize that fact, and be willing and able to apologize when appropriate. One useful thing we can do is to avoid putting labels on things and just see them for what they are. Human beings are composed of a tangled, complicated tapestry of different motivations and urges, and knowing and seeing this can help keep us out of the trap of yes and no thinking, which is a necessaty prerequesite for all these negative emotions.

Another thing we can do along these lines is to ponder the fact that our behavior is not limited to just one or two different options. Remember that the jack and queen of spades can't count over two, so if you come up with three options in any scenario it can help to get you out of that kind of black and white thinking. Sometines it just comes down to brute force. If you're sitting in your car day after day trying not to be angry at your family because they tried to kill you, knowing that these negative emotions need to go, to be transformed, refusing to give into them and praying mightily for those who have wronged you, even though you know you are doing it for your sake much more than theirs, at some point the dawn breaks, the sky opens up, and you start to see the light of dawn.

Another interesting line of observation of oneself is in relation to objecting. We object to all kinds of things, and in this context objecting means an automatic, mechanical negative Emental response to any particular thing we encounter in life. Let's postulate that a highly spititually advanced (remember that spiritual means inner) human should be able to do anything he or she wants, depending on the situation, and what is best for themselves, the other people involved, and everyone in the world. To be able to metaphorically walk on water, so to speak, to rise above the raging storms of daily life. Let's also ponder the idea that a suficiently advanced human being might reach a point where these so-called contradictions started to fade, blur, and vanish into thin air. If you look at the things we object to in life we will see that a lot of the time, probalby almost all the time, it really doesn't matter, in which case we may as well just go along with the other person. Looking carefully at all the things we object to, and on the flip side how much we demand from others, and how unimportant these things really are, can help make a lot of illusory stuff start to fade away and dissappear.

There is nothing in us that is just exactly perfect, so while most of what we are pretty much needs to get thrown out, there is stuff in there that needs to stay and even be encouraged to grow. One such area is financial responsibility. Most people live from paycheck to paycheck, mortgaged to the hilt, barely able to make their monthly payments, and with nothing saved for a rainy day. Everyone should have at least six month's expenses saved and actually probably several times that much. If we look at financial responsibility as something that can be nurtured, or amplified, (The A in Oat) then this becomes a useful and interesting game. This will be seen to be tied in with all kinds of negative emotions, pride, vanity, and self-love especially. But we do have some good habits in there too, we have a place to live and we eat. But these good parts of ourselves need to be amplified a bit, until they function well enough to get us to a more secure financial situation. There are plenty of good things in all of us that could be polished up, nurtured, cared for, and amplified. It is important to look at all parts of ourselves as impartially as possible, get rid of that which is useless or harmfull, and fix up and repair that which is useful.

Harnel-miaznel blending

red words game

Every time you gain a little knowledge and understanding your ability to love and appreciate progress on your inner path will increase.

The higher (understanding) blends with the lower (knowledge) in order to actualize the middle (king of hearts) and thus becomes either higher (king of hearts) for the preceeding lower (knowledge) or lower (king of hearts) for the suceeding higher (understanding).

Self hypnosis games

In the Games we have to formulate correct aims and then figure out how to persue them. We are trying to move from the Emental Level to the Spiritual Level, or in other words to clean up and fix our four lower levels so that our Spiritual Level will functions properly. Proper aims can be long or short range, and it is important to look at long range aims sometimes. We might, for example, say that someday we will be in complete control of our emotions. This is a good and valid aim, but a vey distant one. whatever you imagine it would look like or entail being, by the time you get there things will look a lot different. Perhaps so much different that you wouldn't even use those words to express it. Aims that are closer and more specific are generally a lot more usefull. Examples here.
